## [0.1.0] - Unreleased

### Features

- `InputRecorder<B>`: wraps any backend, taps its `Input` side, and records every event seen
  through `poll_event`, with `start`/`pause`/`stop`/`save` control and a cloneable `RecorderHandle`.
- `.rgrec`: line-delimited JSON read/write for a recorded session (`read`/`write`).
- `replay_live`: forward replay of a recording into a live `Terminal`/`App`, at recorded pace.
- `install_panic_recorder`: auto-saves an in-progress recording on panic, without suppressing the
  original panic output.
