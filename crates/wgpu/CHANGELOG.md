## [0.1.0+retroglyph-wgpu] - unreleased

### Features

- Initial release: a `wgpu` rendering backend implementing `retroglyph_window::Presenter` over
  Vulkan, Metal, and D3D12. Instanced quads, a grid-packed `R8` glyph atlas, GPU layer compositing,
  sub-cell offsets, and (with the `tilesets` feature) an RGBA sprite pass.
