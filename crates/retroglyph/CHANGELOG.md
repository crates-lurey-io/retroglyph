## [0.1.0+retroglyph] - unreleased

### Features

- Initial release: the consumer-facing facade over `retroglyph-core` and its backend/helper
  crates. Re-exports core's game-author-facing modules at the crate root, a `prelude`, and one
  feature-gated module per backend (`crossterm`, `software`, `gl`, `wgpu`, `ui`), plus the curated
  windowed quick-start surface (`WindowConfig`, `PresenterBuilder`, `run_app`, `run_app_on`) from
  `retroglyph-window`.
